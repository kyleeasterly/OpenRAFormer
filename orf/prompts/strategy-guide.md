# Unit effectiveness guide (numbers from the game's own data files)

## How damage works: armor classes

Every weapon has a damage multiplier against each armor class. Using the wrong
unit against the wrong target wastes 80-95% of its damage. The classes:

- **Infantry** (armor None): all foot soldiers.
- **Light**: wheeled/fast vehicles (Hummvee, Nod Buggy, Recon Bike, Artillery, Rocket Launcher, Stealth Tank), aircraft, and sandbag/chain-link barriers.
- **Heavy**: tanks (Light/Medium/Mammoth/Flame), APC, Harvester, MCV.
- **Wood**: EVERY production building — Construction Yard, Power Plants, Refinery, Barracks/Hand of Nod, Weapons Factory/Airstrip, Comms Center.
- **Concrete**: defense structures (Turret, Guard Towers, Obelisk, SAM Site) and concrete barriers.

## What each unit is FOR (damage per second vs Infantry / Buildings / Tanks)

**Anti-infantry specialists — near-useless against anything else:**
- Hummvee $400 (GDI): 10781 vs infantry, **719 vs buildings, 719 vs tanks — 15x less**. Fast infantry-shredder and scout. NEVER send Hummvees to kill a base.
- Nod Buggy $300: 9375 vs infantry, 625 vs buildings/tanks. Same rule.
- Minigunner $100: 1875 vs infantry, 375 vs buildings, 125 vs tanks (a lone Minigunner needs ~6 minutes to kill a Medium Tank). Cheap meat and scouting.
- Apache $1200 (Nod air): 5000 vs infantry, weak vs tanks.

**Building/base destroyers:**
- Flame Tank $600 (Nod): 5769 vs buildings — a Barracks dies in ~10s. Also melts infantry (6308). Terrible vs tanks (1385). The premier Nod base-killer.
- Orca $1200 (GDI air): 5833 vs buildings/vehicles. Strike aircraft; rearm at Helipad between runs.
- Artillery $600 (Nod): 3846 vs buildings at very long range — siege defenses and bases from outside their range. Fragile (Light armor, 7500 HP): keep escorted.
- Rocket Launcher $900 (GDI): long-range (11 cells) rocket volleys, 1500 vs buildings, 2500 vs light vehicles. GDI's siege piece; keep escorted.
- Grenadier $160 (GDI): 1250 vs buildings — the cheap early swarm option for structures.
- Commando $1500: C4 charge destroys ANY building instantly on contact; 6250 vs infantry. Sneaks well; dies to vehicles.
- Engineer $500: doesn't fight — CAPTURES an enemy building on entry. Capturing the enemy Construction Yard is often better than destroying it.

**Anti-armor:**
- Rocket Soldier $300: 1591 vs tanks and 1318 vs buildings for only $300 — the cheapest anti-armor per dollar. Awful vs infantry (318) and slow: escort with Minigunners.
- Medium Tank $900 (GDI): 2500 vs everything armored, 45000 HP. GDI's workhorse — masses of these win games.
- Mammoth Tank $1800 (GDI, needs Repair Facility): 5000 vs buildings/tanks, 87000 HP, plus anti-air/anti-infantry missiles. Slow (46). Self-heals to half.
- Light Tank $750 (Nod): 2083 vs tanks — weaker than a Medium; win by numbers or flanking.
- Recon Bike $500 (Nod): rockets, 2583 vs tanks/buildings at speed 192 — hit-and-run on harvesters and undefended structures.
- Stealth Tank $900 (Nod, late): invisible until it fires; 3214-4286 vs armor/buildings. Raider, not a line unit.

**Not weapons:** Harvester (your economy — protect it, hunt theirs), MCV, APC (armored infantry transport, light AA), Supply Truck, Chinook, Mobile SAM $600 (Nod): **shoots AIR ONLY** — never buy it to fight ground.

## Defenses (armor Concrete — rockets/shells hurt them, bullets/flames don't much)

- Guard Tower $600: 3000 vs infantry — stops infantry rushes, weak vs tanks (900).
- Advanced Guard Tower $1000 (GDI): 5000 vs tanks AND hits aircraft. All-purpose.
- Turret $600 (Nod): 5000 vs tanks/vehicles, weak vs infantry (1000). Anti-armor.
- Obelisk of Light $1500 (Nod, late): 22500 vs everything on the ground — strongest defense in the game, but drains 150 power: build the power first.
- SAM Site $650 (Nod): anti-air only.
- Defenses die to Artillery/Rocket Launcher standing outside their range — a static line without a mobile army loses.

## Composition rules of thumb

- Mix by target: anti-infantry (Hummvee/Buggy/Minigunner) + anti-armor (Rocket Soldier/tanks) + a building-killer (Flame Tank/Artillery/Grenadier) beats a same-cost single-type army.
- Counter what you SEE: enemy massing infantry → Hummvees/Guard Towers. Enemy tanks → Rocket Soldiers/Turrets/Medium Tanks. Enemy turtling behind defenses → Artillery/Rocket Launcher outrange them, or fly over with Orcas.
- Kill economy first: a dead Harvester ($1100 and its income) hurts more than a dead tank. Refineries and harvesters are soft, valuable targets.
- GDI in one line: expensive, durable, direct — Medium/Mammoth push with Rocket Launcher support, Orcas for reach.
- Nod in one line: cheap, fast, sneaky — Buggy/Bike harassment, Flame Tank base-cracks, Artillery sieges, Stealth/Obelisk late.
